# Durable learning

Updated: unknown — set when adopted or changed.

Store concise lessons that should change future decisions. Link to original evidence and decisions. Keep raw results in experiments or evidence records.

No project learning is supplied by this template. Product direction is not proof of demand or product effectiveness. Record actual learning from evidence.

## Learning index

| ID | Learning | Scope and confidence | Evidence | Consequence | Revisit condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use IDs `LRN-001`, `LRN-002`, and so on. These IDs identify rows in this file, not separate records. Add a dated detail paragraph if a row needs more context.

## Rules

- State the population, context, or version for which a lesson holds.
- Link supporting and contrary evidence; distinguish correlation from a causal result.
- Explain what changed: a priority, product behavior, evaluation, strategy, or runbook.
- Record confidence and what would overturn the lesson.
- Preserve superseded or contradicted lessons with their replacement links; do not silently erase them.
- Promote repeated operational failures into runbook or process changes. A lesson without a changed decision or an explicit decision to retain the current approach has not closed the loop.
