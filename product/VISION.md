# Vision

Updated: 2026-09-26 ([STR-002](steering/STR-002-topform-product-model.md)).

## Durable ambition

An open surface for work: Topform takes the form of the work you're doing, instead of making the work take the form of a fixed app ([STR-002](steering/STR-002-topform-product-model.md)). Why this matters, and for whom, is not yet stated by the owner.

## Principles and boundaries

Direction from the owner's product model (the elaboration is not the owner's):

- **Open.** The owner calls it an open surface. What "open" commits to (formats, extensibility, source, data portability) is not yet stated.
- **Markdown as durable state.** Work persists as Markdown.
- **Private runtime.** Work runs in a private microVM runtime.

Unacceptable tradeoffs and boundaries are unknown. Keep product identity canonical in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md).

## Evolution

Material changes to intent should trace to authorized product direction. Preserve the earlier intent in history and link the steering or decision behind the change. Current bets belong in [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md).
