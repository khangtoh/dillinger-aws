# Experiment title

## Protocol — fix before starting

- Linked outcome and opportunity/feature:
- Hypothesis: for which users, what intervention, what expected change, and why:
- Intervention/version and comparison method:
- Primary metric or qualitative evaluation definition:
- Baseline and source:
- Eligible population, allocation, and exposure:
- Duration/sample plan and rationale:
- Success, failure, and inconclusive criteria:
- Guardrails, resource limits, and stopping criteria:
- Verified instrumentation or research method:
- Rollback/stop procedure and existing authority reference:
- Start time, end condition, next check, and wake-up registration status:

## Results — append after observation

- Observation dates, actual sample/exposure, and evidence links:
- Primary result, guardrails, uncertainty, and missing data:
- Confounds, contrary evidence, and protocol deviations:
- Conclusion: supported, not supported, or inconclusive:
- Decision: continue, expand, revise, revert, stop, or gather evidence:
- Linked decision, learning, and updated product/roadmap/runbook:

## Protocol amendments

Preserve original criteria. Date changes, explain their reason, and state how they affect interpretation. Do not rewrite the hypothesis to match results.
